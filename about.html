About

Built by one person, after one phone call.

Her name was Alex. She said she was a virtual AI recruiter — I thought she was setting up the call. She was the call.

A few questions in, I realized I had prepared for the wrong thing. I was trying to have a conversation. That's not what was happening. I didn't know what it was listening for, or how I was being judged.

So I built the other side of that call.

Practice the conversation until the conversation is yours.

— Jason Scott, Founder, Plumlo

Plumlo is built and run by one person, Jason Scott. No team, no outside investors, just a product built directly from a real problem he ran into himself.

Every part of Plumlo, the interview simulation, the scoring, the blog research on how different AI hiring tools actually work, comes from the same place: trying to give candidates the same real, verified picture of what they're about to face that the AI screening them already has.

Verify this

Plumlo's research is verified before publishing, not assumed from secondhand sources. If you're a journalist, researcher, or just skeptical, reach out through the site and Jason will answer directly.
