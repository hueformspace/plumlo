Which AI Interviewer Is This?

Takes 10 seconds  ·  Plumlo

Many candidates are not told clearly that AI will be involved, so the invite email is often the only clue. Paste the sender address, the subject line and any links from your invite, and this checks them against the platforms we have written verified guides for.

This runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere or saved. You only need the sender, subject and links, not your name or anything personal.

How the match works

The checker looks for the name or web address of a platform in what you paste. If it finds one, it shows our guide for that platform. It does not guess from the wording of the email, and it only knows the platforms listed in our guides. A name that is not recognized does not mean there is no AI involved.

Seeing Greenhouse is a special case. It is hiring software that many companies use to run applications, so its name alone does not mean an AI is interviewing you.

If nothing matches

Check three places in the email: the full sender address, the domain of the interview link, and any small "powered by" line in the footer. Platforms often show up there. If you are still unsure, it is reasonable to ask the recruiter whether AI will be part of the interview. Our general guide works for any of them.
