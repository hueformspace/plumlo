Applying at McDonald's? What to Expect from McHire and Olivia

5 min read  ·  Plumlo

If you apply to work at a McDonald's restaurant, you'll most likely go through McHire, where an AI assistant named Olivia handles the first steps. McDonald's describes the process in four stages: capture, screen, assess and interview. Olivia covers the early ones, and a person from the restaurant team does the interview. Here's what to expect and how to get ready.

What McHire is

McHire is the hiring platform McDonald's restaurants use, built by Paradox, the company behind the Olivia assistant. Restaurant managers use the McHire app to post jobs, review candidates Olivia has already screened, schedule interviews automatically, message candidates by text or web, and send offers. On your side, that means a chat with Olivia, often a personality assessment, and then an interview with a person.

The McHire process, per McDonald's
CaptureYour application
ScreenOlivia chat
AssessOften a personality test
InterviewRestaurant team member

What Olivia collects

Olivia gathers your contact details and answers to the restaurant's screening questions. Reporting on a 2025 security incident showed what McHire stores for each applicant: name, email, phone number, address, application status, chat messages and form answers such as the shifts you can work. That last one matters most. Availability is a deciding factor for restaurant hiring, so answer it carefully and accurately.

The personality test

Wired reported in 2025 that about 90% of McDonald's franchisees use McHire to send applicants to a personality test, and 404 Media described a Paradox personality quiz built around a blue avatar. Neither McDonald's nor Paradox publishes how it is scored. Answer honestly and consistently, and don't try to guess an ideal profile. Contradicting yourself across similar questions tends to read worse than any single answer.

Who decides

McDonald's says a member of the restaurant recruitment team completes the interview. Olivia gets you to that conversation. The interview with a person is the part to prepare for.

The 2025 security incident

In a July 2025 report, researchers Ian Carroll and Sam Curry said that a McHire test account used the password 123456 and that an API flaw could expose records for up to 64 million applicants. They reported it, and the flaws were fixed one day after disclosure. If you applied before mid-2025, be alert to messages that claim to be from McDonald's hiring and ask for money or passwords. A real application never asks you to pay.

Five things to do before you apply

1. Know your availability

Days, shifts and start date are among the answers McHire records. Decide them before you start, and keep them consistent everywhere.

2. Keep answers short and on topic

Applicants report that the chatbot struggles with anything outside its script. Answer what it asks. Save detailed questions for the interview.

3. Take the personality test somewhere quiet

Give it your full attention and answer as yourself. Consistency matters more than any single answer.

4. Watch your phone and email

Managers message candidates by text or web through McHire. A missed message can mean a missed interview slot.

5. Practice the interview out loud

The conversation that decides the job is with a person. Paste the McDonald's job posting into Plumlo, answer a short practice interview out loud, and see exactly what landed and what missed before the real one. Plumlo runs in Spanish and 15 other languages.

Common questions

What is McHire?

McHire is the hiring platform McDonald's restaurants use, built by Paradox. McDonald's describes the process as Capture, Screen, Assess and Interview, with Olivia, a virtual recruitment assistant, handling the early steps.

Is Olivia a real person?

No. Olivia is Paradox's AI recruiting assistant. McDonald's calls it a virtual recruitment assistant. It screens applicants and helps schedule interviews.

Who interviews me at McDonald's?

A person. McDonald's says a member of the restaurant recruitment team completes the interview stage. Olivia handles the steps before that.

Is there a personality test on McHire?

Often, yes. Wired reported in 2025 that about 90% of McDonald's franchisees use McHire to collect applicant information and send applicants to a personality test, and 404 Media reported in 2024 that McDonald's used a Paradox personality quiz built around a blue avatar. Answer honestly and consistently. There is no published right answer.

Why can't Olivia answer my question?

Applicants have reported that the chatbot struggles with questions outside its script. For anything specific, like pay or a schedule conflict, ask the restaurant team at your interview or contact the restaurant directly.

Was McHire hacked?

In a July 2025 report, security researchers Ian Carroll and Sam Curry said that a McHire test account used the password 123456 and that an API flaw could expose records for up to 64 million applicants, including names, emails, phone numbers, addresses and chat messages. They reported it, and the flaws were fixed one day after disclosure. Reports found no sign criminals used it first, but could not rule it out.
