AI Interviewer Interrupting You? What to Do

5 min read  ·  Plumlo

You stop to think for a second. The AI starts talking again. Now you've lost your thread and you're answering a question you haven't finished answering.

It feels like a trap. Mostly it's a timing problem, and timing is something you can practice.

Know what you are allowed to say, then practice the timing until it stops throwing you.

· Founder, Plumlo

What one vendor actually says about it

Mercor is the one AI interview platform whose candidate docs spell this out. They say to keep answers brief and avoid long silences, because extended silence can prompt the AI to interject.

The same page gives you two things to say back. If the AI interrupts during a pause, you can tell it you weren't finished. If you did not hear a question, you can ask it to repeat or clarify. Neither is a penalty move. Mercor lists them as normal things a candidate can do.

What vendors publish about pauses and silence

Most other vendors Not published

That last row is the honest part. In the vendor documentation I reviewed, Mercor was the only one that described when its AI jumps in. If your interview is on another platform, assume a pause is the likely trigger, but do not assume the rules are the same.

Do pauses count against you?

Eightfold's candidate guide says its AI Interviewer is designed to focus on the content of your responses and that accent, tone, pauses and facial expressions are not evaluated. That is a reassuring answer for Eightfold interviews. Mercor's docs, by contrast, do not describe any scoring criteria, so there is no published answer either way.

The practical read: a short, natural pause to think is fine. A long, silent gap is the thing most likely to make an AI step in, and that is where the trouble starts.

Five things to do when the AI cuts in

1. Say you were not finished

Calmly, in one sentence: "I wasn't finished, let me complete that." On Mercor this is explicitly allowed. On other platforms it is worth trying, but check your invite's instructions first.

2. Lead with the headline

Our suggestion, not a vendor rule: put your answer in the first sentence, then add detail. If the AI jumps in halfway through, the part that matters has already been said.

3. Fill the gap with a short phrase, not silence

Our suggestion again: a short phrase like "let me think about that for a second" tells the AI you are still working. It is better than three seconds of nothing.

4. Ask for a repeat instead of guessing

Mercor says you can ask the AI to repeat or clarify a question. If you did not catch it, ask.

5. If it keeps happening, check for a technical issue

Repeated interruptions might be audio dropping out, not your pacing. Mercor says to use the Help button in the interview interface for technical problems during an interview.

Practice the part that throws you

Being cut off is much easier to handle when you have already been cut off ten times. Rehearsing in your head does not recreate the feeling. Speaking your answers to something that responds in real time does. Common questions

Why does the AI interviewer keep cutting me off?

Mercor's help docs say extended silence can prompt its AI to interject, and that candidates should keep answers brief and avoid long silences. Most other vendors do not publish how their AI decides when to speak, so a pause is the likeliest trigger but not a confirmed one everywhere.

Can I tell the AI I was not finished?

On Mercor, yes. Its docs say that if the AI interrupts during a pause, you can tell it you weren't finished. If your interview is on another platform, the instructions inside your invite are the source to check.

Does a long pause hurt my score?

It depends on the platform, and most do not say. Eightfold's candidate guide says its AI Interviewer focuses on the content of your responses and that accent, tone, pauses and facial expressions are not evaluated. Mercor's docs do not describe its scoring criteria, so avoid long silences there.

What if I did not hear or understand the question?

Mercor's docs say you can ask the AI to repeat or clarify a question. Do that rather than guessing.

Is the interruption a technical problem?

It might be, if audio drops or the AI talks over you repeatedly. Mercor's docs say that for technical issues during an interview you can use the Help button in the interview interface, and that you can email support with your browser, operating system and what happened.

How do I practice for being interrupted?

Rehearse out loud, not in your head, with something that responds in real time. A voice session with Plumlo, built from your actual job description, lets you practice leading with the answer and recovering when you lose your thread.
