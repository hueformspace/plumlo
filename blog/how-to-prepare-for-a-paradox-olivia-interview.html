Who is Olivia — and why does she matter

Olivia is the AI recruiting assistant built by Paradox, now part of Workday. Workday completed the acquisition on October 1, 2025; trade press reported a price of $1.0 billion in cash, citing an SEC filing, though Workday's own release doesn't state it. Paradox says candidates reach Olivia over SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, and web chat, and that she works for more than 500 companies. When you apply to a job and get a text that feels strangely fast and conversational, it may be Olivia.

Paradox's product pages describe more than screening. They list automated self-scheduling, rescheduling and reminders meant to improve interview show rates, offer management, and automatic onboarding, and they say recruiters decide when a candidate is handed off to a human. What's switched on depends on the employer.

Unlike HireVue, which records your video answers, Olivia is a text and chat conversation, and Paradox says she responds instantly, around the clock. It can feel like a casual exchange even though it's a screening step.

Five things to do before Olivia texts you

1. Read the job description for hard requirements

Paradox says Olivia screens against the job's requirements, so the posting is your best preview of what she'll ask. Before you apply, identify the non-negotiables: availability windows, required certifications, minimum experience, work authorization. Know your honest answers before the conversation starts.

2. Reply when she texts

Paradox says Olivia responds instantly and that scheduling is automated, so the process moves fast. Whether reply speed affects any result isn't published. In practice, a prompt reply keeps you from losing track of a conversation that happens over text. Keep notifications on while you're actively applying.

3. Be specific about availability

Paradox doesn't publish how answers are evaluated, so don't count on a one-word reply being enough. "Yes" is fine for a yes-or-no question. When she asks about availability, spell it out: "Yes, I'm available Monday through Friday from 8am to 6pm and open to weekends" leaves no ambiguity for you or the employer.

4. Don't try to game it

If you say yes to every requirement and can't meet them, the mismatch tends to surface later and wastes both sides' time. Be honest about hard constraints like shift availability or location. It saves everyone the conversation.

5. Practice answering screening-style questions out loud

Olivia works over text, but the questions behind a screening, like availability, experience, and why this role, are ones a human recruiter will also ask. Saying your answers out loud once makes the typed version clearer. The closest preparation you can do is a realistic simulation built from your actual job description, delivered by voice. That's what Plumlo does.

What the conversation looks like, per Paradox

Paradox describes a friendly text conversation over SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, or web chat, with screening in just a few questions and no long application forms. It doesn't publish a standard script, number of questions, or length, and the flow depends on how the employer set it up.

If you qualify, Paradox says candidates can self-schedule inside the same flow, and its product page advertises frontline candidates scheduled in under five minutes. Treat that as Paradox's marketing figure, not a promise for your employer.

Common questions

Is Olivia a real person?

No. Paradox describes Olivia as an AI assistant, a 24/7 concierge for candidates. The conversation can feel human, but it's automated.

What happens if I fail a knockout question?

Paradox says unqualified candidates are dispositioned or pointed to other roles. What you see, and whether you can reapply, depends on the employer. Paradox doesn't publish a standard message or reapply policy.

Can I ask Olivia questions?

Yes. Paradox says Olivia responds to candidate questions instantly, any time of day. What she can answer depends on how the employer set her up, and Paradox says recruiters decide when a candidate is handed off to a human.

Does Olivia score my answers?

Paradox says she screens candidates against the job's requirements. It doesn't publish how individual answers are evaluated, whether reply speed counts, or how many questions to expect. Treat any guide that states exact scoring rules as unverified.

How long does an Olivia screening take?

Paradox says screening happens in just a few questions and that screening and scheduling that used to take days or weeks now happens in minutes. It doesn't publish a standard number of questions or minutes, so check what your employer tells you.

How is Olivia different from HireVue?

HireVue is a one-way video format where your responses are recorded and submitted to the recruiter. Olivia is a text and chat conversation that starts as soon as you apply. Which one you meet depends on the employer.
