Who is Olivia — and why does she matter

Olivia is the AI recruiting assistant built by Paradox, now part of Workday following a $1 billion acquisition that closed in October 2025. She operates via text, SMS, WhatsApp, and chat — and she's already working for more than 500 companies around the world. When you apply to a job and get a text that feels strangely fast and conversational, there's a good chance that's Olivia.

Unlike HireVue — which records video and scores it later — Olivia works in real time. She texts back immediately. She asks follow-up questions based on what you say. She decides on the spot whether you move forward or not. Most candidates don't realize they're being screened until it's already over.

What Olivia is actually screening for

Olivia isn't evaluating your personality or your tone. She's running a structured qualification filter based on the job's requirements. The questions are designed to pass or fail you on hard criteria — quickly, at scale, without a human in the loop.

Answer "No" to a knockout question and the conversation ends. Olivia will send a polite message — but you won't move forward. These aren't trick questions. They're binary filters. Know which ones apply to your role before you reply.

Five things to do before Olivia texts you

1. Read the job description for hard requirements

Every knockout question Olivia asks comes directly from the role requirements. Before you apply, identify the non-negotiables — availability windows, required certifications, minimum experience, work authorization. These are the questions she'll lead with. Know your honest answers before the conversation starts.

2. Respond immediately when she texts

Olivia tracks how quickly you respond. Slow replies signal low interest. The screening is often triggered within minutes of your application — if you don't respond within a few hours, some employers route you to a lower priority queue. Keep notifications on when you're actively applying.

3. Give complete answers, not one-word replies

Olivia's system reads your responses for completeness. "Yes" answers basic availability — "Yes, I'm available Monday through Friday from 8am to 6pm and open to weekends" scores better because it's specific and leaves no ambiguity. Give her enough to work with.

4. Don't try to game it

Candidates who answer "Yes" to every requirement and later can't meet them create problems downstream. Olivia passes you forward — but the human interview exposes the mismatch and you've wasted both sides' time. Be honest about hard constraints like shift availability or location. It saves everyone the conversation.

5. Practice answering screening questions out loud

Olivia also conducts voice-based screening for some employers. If you get a call rather than a text, the same logic applies — clear, specific, confident answers to structured questions. The best preparation is a realistic simulation where you practice answering under pressure before it counts.

What the conversation actually looks like

Olivia opens with something like: "Hi, I'm Olivia — your recruiting assistant for [Company]. I have a few quick questions to see if this role is a great fit. Ready to get started?" It feels like texting a real person. The language is natural, not robotic. That's by design.

She'll move through three to six questions — usually eligibility first, then availability, then role-specific criteria. Each answer you give can trigger a follow-up. The whole conversation typically takes five to ten minutes.

If you qualify, she schedules your next step automatically — often a human interview or an assessment — right inside the same thread. No email, no back-and-forth. The speed is the point.

Common questions

Is Olivia a real person?

No. Olivia is Paradox's AI recruiting assistant. She's named after the founder's wife and uses a real photo as her profile picture — which surprises a lot of candidates. The conversation feels human, but it's fully automated.

What happens if I fail a knockout question?

Olivia sends a polite message saying you're not a fit for this role right now, and may invite you to explore other positions. You won't move to the next stage. Some companies allow you to reapply after a set period.

Can I ask Olivia questions?

Yes. Olivia is configured to answer FAQs about the role — pay range, benefits, shift details, application status. She has a library of responses built by the employer. If she can't answer something, she'll let you know a recruiter will follow up.

Does Olivia do voice interviews too?

Some employers use Paradox for voice-based screening in addition to text. If you receive a call from an AI recruiter early in the process, that may be Olivia operating in voice mode. The same preparation applies — clear, specific, complete answers.

How is Olivia different from HireVue?

HireVue records your video answers and scores them after the fact — it's asynchronous. Olivia is a live conversation via text or voice that qualifies you in real time. HireVue tends to appear at mid-size to enterprise companies in finance and tech. Olivia is dominant in high-volume hiring — retail, food service, healthcare, and logistics.
