What HeyMilo actually is

HeyMilo is a conversational AI interviewer built for high-volume hiring, reaching candidates across text, WhatsApp, email, phone calls, and video. Most interviews are on-demand rather than scheduled, so you complete it when it's convenient rather than waiting for an appointment slot.

The interview itself starts with a few knockout questions to confirm basic qualification, then moves into an adaptive conversation that probes deeper into your actual skills and experience, adjusting based on what you say rather than following a fixed script.

Where you're likely to run into it

HeyMilo is especially common in high-volume, frontline hiring, retail, restaurants, healthcare, warehouse roles, and skilled trades, where employers need to screen large numbers of applicants quickly and consistently. It integrates with major ATS platforms including Greenhouse, Workday, and SmartRecruiters, so it often shows up as the first real step after applying.

What HeyMilo is actually listening for

Every response gets scored against defined criteria, with a transcript and recording kept alongside a written rationale for each score. Genuinely worth knowing: HeyMilo has its scoring system audited on a recurring basis by an independent AI assurance company, and posts those results to a public dashboard rather than only claiming fairness privately. That doesn't change what you should do in the interview, but it does mean the scoring is more accountable than most.

What the AI evaluates
Passing early knockout questions clearly High weight
Specific, relevant skills and experience High weight

Which channel you complete it on Not scored

Since the platform runs active cheat detection and generates a trust score for every interview, the safest approach is simply answering as yourself, consistently, rather than anything that could read as scripted or completed with outside help.

Five things to do before your HeyMilo interview

1. Don't assume "on-demand" means low stakes

Being able to complete it on your own time is a convenience, not a sign the evaluation is casual. Treat it exactly like a scheduled interview once you start.

2. Nail the early knockout questions

These come first and confirm basic qualification before the deeper conversation even begins. Know the plain facts about your availability, experience, and qualifications cold before you start.

3. Practice speaking specific answers out loud

Since the conversation adapts based on what you actually say, vague answers tend to get followed up on. Practice saying specific, real examples out loud rather than just thinking through general points.

4. Pick whichever channel you're most comfortable with

Since HeyMilo supports text, phone, and video, choose the format where you naturally communicate best, there's no evidence one channel scores better than another, only that you'll perform better in the one that suits you.

5. Practice with an AI that scores your answers

The closest preparation available is a live simulation built from your actual job description, delivered by voice, and scored on what you actually say. That's what Plumlo does. One session beforehand changes how ready you feel walking into an on-demand interview with no fixed start time to build up to.

What to expect on the day

A message by text, WhatsApp, or email with a link to start when you're ready, or a direct call depending on how the employer has it configured. Expect early qualifying questions first, then a longer adaptive conversation. Complete it somewhere quiet with a stable connection, since it's designed to feel like a real conversation, not a form to fill out.

Common questions

What is a HeyMilo trust score?

HeyMilo runs multi-layered cheat detection during the interview and generates a trust score reflecting the likelihood the interview was genuine, not scripted or completed by someone else. A high, verified trust score simply confirms the conversation was real.

Does HeyMilo actually check its AI for bias?

Yes, and unusually, it publishes the results. HeyMilo partners with an independent AI assurance company to run recurring third-party bias audits, following requirements like NYC Local Law 144, and posts the outcomes to a public dashboard rather than only making a private claim of fairness.

How does HeyMilo contact candidates?

It reaches out across multiple channels, including text, WhatsApp, email, phone calls, and video, often letting candidates choose whichever channel is easiest for them to complete the interview on.

Is a HeyMilo interview scheduled or on-demand?

Most HeyMilo interviews are on-demand rather than scheduled, meaning candidates can complete them when convenient rather than waiting for a specific appointment time.

What industries use HeyMilo most?

HeyMilo is especially common in high-volume, frontline hiring, retail, restaurants, healthcare, warehouse roles, and skilled trades, where speed and consistent screening across large applicant pools matters most.
