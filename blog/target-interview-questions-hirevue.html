Target Interview Questions and the HireVue Video Interview

5 min read  ·  Plumlo

Target publishes its own sample interview questions, and for some roles the first interview is a recorded video through HireVue. Here are the five questions from Target's interview guide, how the video interview works step by step, and how to practice out loud before yours.

The five questions Target publishes

Target's interview prep guide ties each sample question to one thing it is checking. The real questions vary by role, but these show what Target cares about.

Target's sample questions and what each checks
Your background and experience for this jobJob knowledge
Working with people who think differentlyInclusivity
A time you helped solve a problemProblem solving
How you build working relationshipsConnection
How you make your work succeedDrive

How to answer them

Target asks you to share a real story and the steps you took, and to tie each answer to the skills and values in the question. One specific example with what you did and how it turned out beats a general description of yourself. For live interviews, Target suggests aiming for about five minutes per question, so prepare stories with enough detail to fill that without wandering.

The HireVue recorded video interview

Target says some business areas use a recorded video system. The invitation comes from interviews@hirevue.com, signed Target Careers, with the subject line "Congrats! You've been selected to interview with Target." If you don't see it, Target says to wait more than three hours after the selection email before searching for it.

What Target says about the interview itself:

  • Plan for about 30 minutes. You can stop and return with the same link.
  • Watch the introduction video all the way through. It can't be replayed.
  • Practice questions are unlimited and private. Only you see them, so use them to check your light and sound.
  • Watch the time you are given for each answer.
  • Any smartphone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone works. On a computer, Target recommends Chrome.
  • A hiring manager or recruiter reviews your answers along with your application.

On retakes, Target's page contradicts itself: one line says you will have a chance to re-record, and its FAQ says Target does not allow retakes. Plan for one take.

The rest of Target's hiring process

Every application goes through Workday, sometimes with a short job-skills assessment or availability questions. A recruiter may contact you by phone or email. Interviews can be by phone or video, in person, or recorded, and you may get more than one. Offers arrive in your Workday candidate homepage. Target says it aims to answer within five business days of your video, and to contact your recruiter or the store's HR after ten.

One warning from Target itself: it will never ask for personal information through messages. Apply only through Target's careers site or Workday.

Five things to do before your Target interview

1. Prepare one story for each of the five questions

Write down a real example for each: what happened, what you did, how it turned out.

2. Read the job description for the skills it names

Target asks you to connect answers to the skills and values in the question. The job posting tells you which ones matter for this role.

3. Use every practice question in HireVue

They are unlimited and private. Since you may get only one take on the real questions, this is where you get comfortable.

4. Answer on your own

Target says answers must reflect your own judgment, without outside AI help during the interview. Practicing before is how you get ready.

5. Practice the answers out loud

Paste the Target job posting into Plumlo, answer a short practice interview out loud, and see exactly what landed and what missed. It also runs in Spanish and 14 other languages. Then read our HireVue guide for how the platform itself works.

Common questions

What questions does Target ask in an interview?

Target's own interview prep guide lists five sample questions: your background and experience for the job, how you work with people who think differently, a time you solved a problem, how you build working relationships, and how you make sure your work succeeds. Target notes the actual questions vary by role.

Does Target use HireVue?

Yes, for recorded video interviews. Target says some business areas use a recorded video system, and the invitation comes from interviews@hirevue.com with the subject line Congrats! You've been selected to interview with Target.

Can you retake a Target video interview?

Plan for one take. Target's recorded video page says you will have a chance to re-record, but its FAQ on the same page says Target does not allow retakes. Use the unlimited, private practice questions first instead.

How long is the Target video interview?

Target says to plan for about 30 minutes. You can stop and come back with the same link, but finish it promptly: an incomplete interview means you are no longer considered.

When will Target get back to me?

Target aims to respond within five business days of receiving your video. If it has been more than ten days, contact your recruiter or the store's HR.

Can I use AI to help during a Target interview?

No. Target says responses must reflect your own understanding and judgment, without external AI-generated help. Approved accommodations and assistive technologies are the exception. Practicing beforehand is fine.
