Applying at Chipotle? What to Expect from Ava Cado

5 min read  ·  Plumlo

If you apply for a restaurant job at Chipotle, the first one to talk to you will probably be Ava Cado, Chipotle's AI hiring assistant. Chipotle says Ava chats with candidates, answers questions about the company, collects basic information, schedules interviews for hiring managers and sends offers to the people managers pick. Here's what that means for you and how to get ready.

What Ava Cado is

Ava Cado is a conversational hiring assistant built by Paradox, the same company behind the Olivia assistant used by McDonald's and many other employers. Chipotle announced it in October 2024 and rolled it out across its more than 3,500 restaurants in North America and Europe. Chipotle calls Ava a virtual team member, and it speaks English, Spanish, French and German.

What Ava does, per Chipotle
Answers your questionsAbout Chipotle
Collects basic informationAbout you
Schedules interviewsFor hiring managers
Sends offersTo candidates managers select

Is the chat with Ava the interview?

It can be your first conversation in the process. CBS News reported that a candidate's first interview could be with Ava rather than a hiring manager. But Chipotle's own description puts people in charge of the decision: Ava schedules interviews for hiring managers and only sends offers to candidates the managers select. As Chipotle COO Jason Kidd said at Fortune's COO Summit in June 2026, "At the end of the day, that applicant ends up working for a human."

So treat Ava as the front door. Clear, accurate answers get you to the interview with a manager, and that conversation is the one to prepare for.

How fast it moves

Chipotle's COO said the time from application to first day has dropped from 12 days to four, and that about 30% of scheduling now happens after hours, because Ava works around the clock. Expect messages outside normal business hours, and expect interview slots to go quickly.

What Chipotle doesn't publish

Chipotle and Paradox don't publish the exact questions Ava asks or every channel it uses to reach you. Check your application confirmation for how the conversation starts. For how Paradox assistants work in general, including what a real Paradox link looks like, see our Paradox Olivia guide.

Five things to do before you apply

1. Have your basics ready

Ava collects basic information. Know your availability, the hours you can work and when you can start before you begin, so the chat moves without guesswork.

2. Reply quickly

Hiring now takes days, not weeks, and Ava schedules around the clock. A late reply can mean a later interview slot.

3. Use the language you're strongest in

Ava speaks English, Spanish, French and German. Answer in the one where you explain yourself best.

4. Ask Ava your questions

Chipotle says Ava answers questions about the company. Use it for the practical ones, like what the role involves or what happens next, before you sit down with a manager.

5. Practice the manager interview out loud

Common questions

Is Ava Cado a real person?

No. Ava Cado is Chipotle's AI hiring assistant, a conversational system built by Paradox, the company behind the Olivia hiring assistant. Chipotle describes Ava as a virtual team member that chats with candidates.

Is the chat with Ava Cado the interview?

It can be your first conversation in the process. CBS News reported that a candidate's first interview could be with Ava. Chipotle says Ava collects basic information and schedules interviews for hiring managers, and only sends offers to candidates the managers select.

Does a person decide if I get hired at Chipotle?

Yes, according to Chipotle. Its October 2024 announcement says Ava sends offers to candidates that managers have selected. Chipotle COO Jason Kidd put it this way in June 2026: the applicant ends up working for a human.

What languages does Ava Cado speak?

English, Spanish, French and German, per Chipotle's announcement. Pick the language you answer in most clearly.

How long does it take to get hired at Chipotle now?

Chipotle's COO said in June 2026 that the time from application to first day dropped from 12 days to four. Chipotle had said in 2024 it expected Ava to cut time to hire for restaurant roles by as much as 75%.

Who built Ava Cado?

Paradox, the conversational hiring company. Paradox and Chipotle both name Paradox as the platform behind Ava.
