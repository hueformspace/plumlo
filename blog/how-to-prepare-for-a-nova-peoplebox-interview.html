How to Prepare for a Nova (Peoplebox) AI Interview in 2026

6 min read  ·  Plumlo

I didn't build Plumlo because AI interviewing is a bad idea. I built it because nobody warns you it's happening, or tells you what it's actually listening for.

What Nova actually is

Nova is Peoplebox's AI interviewer. Peoplebox says it listens and responds to what a candidate actually says, with adaptive follow-up questions, so it's closer to talking with a sharp human interviewer than reading answers into a camera. It runs by phone, video, or chat, and on the phone Nova calls you directly.

What sets Nova apart is speed and verification. It's built to reach candidates without waiting on a recruiter's calendar, and Peoplebox says identity verification, deepfake detection, and behavior monitoring run silently throughout the interview.

Where you're likely to run into it

Peoplebox says more than 600 companies use it, from fast-growing startups to large employers, and names customers including Razorpay, Ripple, KPMG, Veriff, and JioHotstar, with testimonials from Sprinto and Neurealm. It connects to applicant tracking systems including Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and Ashby, so it often shows up early and fast in a hiring process rather than as a later-stage formal interview.

What Peoplebox says Nova evaluates

Peoplebox says Nova scores role competency, skills, culture fit, and communication, and gives the hiring team a hire or no-hire recommendation, red flags, and an integrity score, with a full transcript available. Employers control the skills, depth, difficulty, and structure of the interview, and Nova can learn from the company's own job descriptions, past interviews, and ATS notes. Peoplebox doesn't publish how each area is weighted.

Because Nova follows up on what you just said, a vague or rehearsed answer tends to get probed directly. That part is advice, not a published rule: specific answers tied to real experience hold up much better under that kind of follow-up.

Five things to do before your Nova interview

1. Be ready before you apply, not after

Since Nova can reach out as soon as you apply, there may be no gap to prepare in afterward. Do your prep before you hit submit, not while you wait for a scheduled slot that might not exist.

2. Know your own resume cold

Nova is built around the role and the employer's own hiring data, and it follows up on what you say. Be ready to speak specifically to what's actually on your resume, since vague answers that don't match your background stand out quickly.

3. Prepare for real follow-up, not just your main answer

Practice not just your first answer but what you'd say if immediately asked to go deeper. That follow-up is very likely coming.

4. Be yourself, consistently

Peoplebox says identity verification, deepfake detection, and behavior monitoring run throughout the interview. Answer as yourself, in a normal setting, without anything that could read as an attempt to fake or outsource your responses.

5. Practice with an AI that scores your answers

The closest preparation available is a live simulation built from your actual job description, delivered by voice, and scored on what you actually say. That's what Plumlo does. One session before an instant-start interview like this changes how ready you feel the moment it happens.

What to expect on the day

The interview may begin soon after you apply, with no separate scheduling step, by phone, video, or chat depending on the employer. Expect a live, adaptive conversation with real follow-up questions, and identity checks running in the background. Find a quiet, well-lit space before you apply, since there may be little advance notice.

Common questions

Why did my interview start right after I applied?

Peoplebox says Nova syncs with the employer's applicant tracking system the moment a candidate applies, then reaches out and runs the interview, with no scheduling. That's a deliberate design choice to speed up hiring, not a glitch.

Does Nova check for deepfakes and identity fraud?

Yes. Peoplebox says identity verification, deepfake detection, and behavior monitoring run silently throughout the interview, and the hiring team gets an integrity score alongside the other scores.

Is Nova a real conversation or a recorded video?

It's a two-way conversation, not a one-way recorded video. Peoplebox says Nova listens and responds to what you actually say, with adaptive follow-up questions, by phone, video, or chat.

What does the hiring team get after a Nova interview?

Peoplebox says the hiring team gets scores on role competency, skills, culture fit, and communication, a hire or no-hire recommendation, red flags, an integrity score, and a full transcript.

Can I prepare for a Nova interview?

Yes, and since it can start immediately after you apply, most of the useful preparation has to happen before you hit submit, not after. Practicing your answers out loud ahead of time matters more here than with an interview you schedule days in advance.
