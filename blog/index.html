Roughly 15 to 25 minutes, recorded and scored, with a single use link.
Eightfold AI Interviewer
Scores the content of your answers, not your accent, tone or facial expressions, but it needs your camera and microphone.
iMocha Tara
Live, adaptive voice or chat interviews in 30 languages, inside iMocha's skills platform.
Alex (formerly Apriora)
Interviews candidates by phone, video, SMS and WhatsApp. This is the employer product, not the practice interviewer on this site.
Tenzo AI
Live voice screens by phone and video, scored against a rubric the employer sets for the role.
ConverzAI (Recruiter Jamie)
Calls, texts or emails within minutes of applying through a staffing agency.
Nova (Peoplebox)
Starts the interview the instant you apply, and checks for deepfakes to verify you are really you.
Purplefish
Fast, high volume phone screens built for basic qualification questions.
Greenhouse
How structured hiring works at thousands of companies and what interviewers score.
